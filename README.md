## <samp>`01.` &nbsp;whoami</samp>

**Co-founder of [Kyber Tech](https://somoskyber.com.br)** — a technology studio building websites, automation, applied AI and digital products. I'm involved end to end: discovering what the client actually needs, defining the solution, building it, shipping it and staying in the relationship afterwards.

Alongside that, I'm a **Full Stack Developer at Minsait — Grupo Indra (Espanha)**, where I build and modernize large-scale enterprise systems for **public healthcare, traffic and defense**, moving between legacy constraints and modern architectures without losing the business rules that already work.

Right now I contribute to the **modernization of SINGRA 2 for the Brazilian Navy** — migrating a legacy Angular + DWR frontend to a modern Angular stack with REST/JSON APIs and JWT Bearer auth, and bringing the backend up to current Java and Spring Boot while preserving the service, business and persistence layers.

In my own products I point the same engineering discipline at **privacy, performance, accessibility and measurable quality**. Everything ships with tests, Lighthouse scores and real numbers attached.
